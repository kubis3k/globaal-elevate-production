## Where we came from

Globaal Elevate Production didn't start in an office. It started at events in northern Bohemia — music, people, experiences. From that foundation, the projects that now form our portfolio gradually grew.

Every brand under GEP solves a problem we experienced ourselves. Globaal Production because good branding was out of reach for small businesses. Vision Technology because we were paying for code we could build better and cheaper ourselves.

## What we do today

Today we bring together four brands:

- **3lwave** — next-generation event platform
- **Globaal Records** — independent music label
- **Globaal Production** — creative and marketing agency for SMBs
- **Vision Technology** — custom development of AI systems, websites and digital products

## Where we're heading

We're based in Prague but rooted in the north. We want to build projects with real foundations — not promising pitch decks, but genuine value for the people who use them.
